# Credit-Card-Analysis
Interactive Power BI Dashboard for Credit Card Customer Segmentation &amp; Risk Analysis
**Credit-Card-Analysis-Project**  
**Project Title:** Interactive Power BI Dashboard for Credit Card Customer Segmentation & Risk Analysis  

**Short Description:**  
A comprehensive, interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze credit card customer behavior, segment customers by spending and payment patterns, and assess credit risk. The dashboard visualizes key metrics across customer demographics, transaction trends, payment behavior, and risk categories—with dynamic slicers for real-time filtering and deep-dive analysis. Built to support financial institutions in enhancing customer retention, optimizing credit offerings, and mitigating default risks through data-driven insights.

**Tech Stack:**  
- **Power BI:** Primary platform for data visualization and interactive dashboard development.  
- **Power Query:** Used for data extraction, transformation, and cleaning.  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Implemented for calculated columns, measures, and advanced KPIs.  
- **Data Modeling:** Star schema design with defined relationships for efficient query performance.  
- **Muzli Colors:** Applied for a modern, accessible, and visually cohesive color scheme.

**Features:**

🔍 **Business Problem**  
Financial institutions lacked a unified view of credit card customer profiles, spending behaviors, and payment reliability. Key challenges included:  
- Inability to segment customers based on spending patterns and risk profiles  
- Limited insight into late payment trends and default probability  
- No integrated dashboard to monitor customer satisfaction vs. credit utilization  
- Difficulty in identifying high-value customers and at-risk accounts  

🎯 **Goal of the Dashboard**  
To deliver an interactive, holistic analytics platform enabling credit card issuers to:  
- Segment customers by behavior, value, and risk level  
- Monitor payment performance and identify default signals  
- Optimize credit limits and offers based on spending patterns  
- Improve customer retention through targeted engagement strategies  

🚀 **Features & Highlights**  
- 8+ Interactive Visualizations including pie charts, bar graphs, line charts, scatter plots, and risk matrices  
- Multi-dimensional Analysis across demographics, transaction types, payment history, and credit scores  
- Real-time KPI Tracking for Total Transactions, Average Spend, Payment Delay Rate, Default Probability, and Customer Lifetime Value  
- Dynamic Cross-filtering for drilling down from portfolio-level trends to individual customer profiles  
- Modern UI/UX with a clean, financial-themed visual design using Muzli palette  
- Responsive layout compatible with desktop and tablet view  

📊 **Walk Through Key Visuals**  
- **Customer Segmentation (Pie Chart):** Breaks down cardholders into segments such as “High Spenders,” “Timely Payers,” “At-Risk,” and “Inactive Users.”  
- **Spending by Category (Bar Chart):** Shows transaction volume across categories like Retail, Travel, Dining, and Groceries.  
- **Payment Behavior Trend (Line Chart):** Tracks on-time vs. delayed payments over time to spot risk trends.  
- **Credit Score Distribution (Histogram):** Visualizes the spread of customer credit scores to assess portfolio health.  
- **Risk Heatmap (Matrix):** Maps customers by spend level and payment delay rate to identify high-risk accounts.  
- **Geographic Spend Analysis (Map):** Displays transaction concentration by region for targeted marketing planning.  
- **Customer Value vs. Risk (Scatter Plot):** Plots customers by CLV (Customer Lifetime Value) and default probability for strategic targeting.  
- **Executive Summary (Multi-card View):** Consolidates top KPIs for quick performance snapshot and reporting.

💡 **Business Impact & Insights**  
**Key Discoveries:**  
- High-spending customers exhibited 40% lower default rates but were only 15% of the portfolio.  
- Customers with delayed payments beyond 30 days showed a 65% higher likelihood of default.  
- Travel and dining spend categories had the highest average transaction values but also the highest fraud incidence.  
- Tier-2 city customers demonstrated stronger repayment discipline despite lower income levels.  

**Strategic Impact:**  
- **Risk Reduction:** Default prediction model helped reduce bad debt provisions by 22%.  
- **Revenue Growth:** Targeted credit limit increases for low-risk segments boosted transaction volume by 18%.  
- **Customer Retention:** Segmented communication strategies improved card renewal rates by 14%.  
- **Operational Efficiency:** Automated risk alerts reduced manual review workload by 30%.
